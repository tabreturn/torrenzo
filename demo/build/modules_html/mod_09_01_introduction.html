Module 9.1: Introduction to Digital Curation

Introduction

A collection is more than the sum of its items. Curating a digital collection means making intentional decisions about selection, organisation, presentation, and interpretation. Each choice – which fruits to include, how to sequence them, what metadata to attach, what story to tell – shapes how a viewer experiences your exhibition.

This module introduces the principles of digital curation and metadata management. You will learn how to structure a collection so that it is browseable, searchable, and narratively coherent. These skills are directly applicable to Assessment 3, where you will curate a digital fruit exhibition as a paired project.[1]

Subject Learning Outcomes

This module will help you achieve the following outcomes:

  • (c) Organize a digital collection of fruit images and notes, applying a consistent tagging scheme for easy reference.
  • (g) Curate a digital collection with consistent metadata, tagging, and visual presentation standards.
  • (h) Present a curated fruit exhibition that communicates sensory, cultural, and botanical insights.

Assessment Progression

This module is directly aligned with Assessment 3. The metadata system you design in this module will form the backbone of your exhibition's organisation. The curatorial principles you learn will guide your selection, sequencing, and presentation decisions.

Work through the activities with your exhibition partner. The decisions you make together now will save you time and ensure consistency later.
