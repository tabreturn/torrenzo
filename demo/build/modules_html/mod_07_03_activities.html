Resources

The Global History of Fruit (Mwangi & dos Santos, 2022)

This comprehensive volume traces the history of cultivated fruits from their wild origins to their current global distribution. Chapters on mango, banana, pineapple, and durian provide detailed case studies that connect botany, trade history, and cultural studies.

Access The Global History of Fruit

Fruit in Art and Visual Culture (Okafor, 2021)

This richly illustrated book surveys the representation of fruit in art from ancient mosaics to contemporary photography. The chapter on tropical fruit in colonial and postcolonial art is particularly relevant to understanding how visual representations shape cultural perceptions of fruit and the places they come from.

Access Fruit in Art and Visual Culture

Food, Memory, and Migration (Park, 2023)

This sociological study explores the role of food – including fruit – in the lives of migrant and diaspora communities. The author argues that familiar foods serve as "edible memories," connecting migrants to their places of origin and helping to construct cultural identity in new contexts.

Access Food, Memory, and Migration

Practice Activity

Choose one of the following activities:

Option A: Cultural Fruit Profile

Select a tropical fruit and write a 300-word cultural profile suitable for a museum exhibition label. Include:

  • Botanical information (family, origin, growing regions)
  • Cultural significance (symbolism, traditions, rituals)
  • Historical journey (how it travelled and who carried it)
  • Contemporary context (how it is consumed and understood today)
  • One striking visual detail or story that makes this fruit memorable

Option B: Visual Analysis

Find three artworks or advertisements that feature tropical fruit. Analyse how each representation shapes the viewer's understanding of the fruit. What is emphasised? What is omitted? How does the visual language reflect the cultural context in which the image was created?

Option C: Personal Reflection

Write a 300-word reflection on a fruit that holds personal or cultural significance for you. What memories or associations does it carry? How does your personal experience relate to the broader cultural history of this fruit?
