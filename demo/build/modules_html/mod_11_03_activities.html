Resources

Curating Digital Exhibitions: Best Practices (Washington & Tan, 2023)

This guide synthesises lessons from museum studies and digital humanities to present best practices for curating small-scale digital exhibitions. The chapter on exhibition text writing is particularly useful for refining your entries and curator's statement.

Access Curating Digital Exhibitions

Peer Review and Constructive Feedback (Nguyen, 2021)

Giving and receiving feedback is a professional skill in its own right. This short guide offers frameworks for providing constructive, specific, and actionable feedback on creative and curatorial work.

Access Peer Review and Constructive Feedback

Reflective Writing for Creative Projects (Park, 2022)

This guide to reflective writing covers techniques for moving beyond description into genuine critical reflection. It includes examples of strong and weak reflections with annotations explaining what makes the difference.

Access Reflective Writing for Creative Projects

Exhibition Showcase

In your final facilitated session, you will present your exhibition to the class. Prepare:

  1. A 2-minute introduction: Your exhibition title, your curatorial approach, and one or two highlights
  2. A guided tour: Walk your classmates through your exhibition, pointing out your strongest entries and explaining your sequencing choices
  3. A Q&A: Be prepared to answer questions about your selection, collaboration, and design decisions

Use feedback from the showcase to make final refinements before submitting.

Subject Reflection

As you complete this subject, take a moment to reflect on your learning journey:

  • What surprised you most about studying tropical fruits?
  • Which skill – observation, description, comparison, photography, curation – do you feel most confident about?
  • How might you apply these skills in other areas of your study or work?
  • If you were to continue studying fruit documentation, what would you want to explore next?

Your responses to these questions will not be assessed, but reflecting on your learning helps consolidate your skills and identify areas for future development.
