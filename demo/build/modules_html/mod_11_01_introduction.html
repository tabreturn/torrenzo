Module 11.1: Bringing It All Together

Introduction

Over the past eleven modules, you have developed a comprehensive set of skills: sensory observation, structured comparison, cultural research, photography, metadata management, and digital curation. This final module helps you integrate these skills into a cohesive exhibition that demonstrates everything you have learned.

Your digital exhibition is not just a collection of fruit entries. It is a portfolio that showcases your ability to observe carefully, describe vividly, organise systematically, and present professionally. These are transferable skills that extend far beyond tropical fruit studies.[1]

Subject Learning Outcomes

This module will help you achieve the following outcomes:

  • (g) Curate a digital collection with consistent metadata, tagging, and visual presentation standards.
  • (h) Present a curated fruit exhibition that communicates sensory, cultural, and botanical insights.

Assessment Progression

This module is designed to be completed alongside Assessment 3. Use the activities to workshop your exhibition with your partner, refine your entries, and prepare your curator's reflection.

The final facilitated session for this module is an exhibition showcase. You and your partner will present your exhibition to the class, explaining your curatorial choices and receiving feedback before final submission.
