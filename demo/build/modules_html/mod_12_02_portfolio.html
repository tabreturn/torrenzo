Module 12.2: Skills Portfolio

Documenting Your Learning Journey

This module includes no new content or assessment tasks. Instead, it provides space to organise and reflect on the work you have produced throughout the subject. Consider compiling a personal skills portfolio that includes:

  • Your favourite sensory description from any module
  • Your comparison table from Assessment 2
  • A selection of photographs from your exhibition
  • Your curator's reflection from Assessment 3
  • Notes on what you would do differently next time

Peer Showcase

In your final facilitated session, you will have the opportunity to share highlights from your exhibition and hear what other pairs have created. This is a celebration of the work you have done — not an assessment, but a chance to see the full range of creative approaches your cohort has taken.

Prepare to share:

  1. One entry from your exhibition that you are particularly proud of
  2. One challenge you encountered and how you solved it
  3. One thing you learned that surprised you

Beyond This Subject

The documentation and curation skills you have developed are directly applicable to:

  • Academic research: Literature reviews, data organisation, visual documentation
  • Creative practice: Portfolio curation, project documentation, visual storytelling
  • Professional work: Knowledge management, content organisation, presentation design
  • Personal projects: Travel journals, recipe collections, garden records

Consider how you might apply these skills in your next project, whatever it may be.

Subject Feedback

Your feedback helps improve this subject for future students. Please take a few minutes to complete the subject evaluation survey, available through the LMS. What worked well? What could be improved? What surprised you? Your honest responses are valued and will be used to refine the subject content and structure.
