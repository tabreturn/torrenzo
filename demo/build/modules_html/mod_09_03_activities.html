Module 9.3: Activities

Resources

Introduction to Digital Curation (Bennett, 2022)

This accessible introduction covers the fundamentals of curating digital collections, from metadata standards to user experience design. The chapter on exhibition narrative is particularly useful for thinking about how to structure your ten-fruit sequence.

Access Introduction to Digital Curation

Metadata for Cultural Collections (Washington, 2021)

This guide introduces Dublin Core and other metadata standards used by museums, libraries, and archives. While your fruit exhibition does not need to adopt a formal metadata standard, understanding how professional institutions approach metadata will help you design a robust tagging system.

Access Metadata for Cultural Collections

Designing Browseable Collections (Okonkwo, 2023)

This article examines how users browse digital collections and what design features encourage exploration and discovery. The author presents case studies of successful digital exhibitions and extracts principles that you can apply to your own project.

Access Designing Browseable Collections

Practice Activity

Create a prototype of your exhibition's tag index or filter system. Using simple HTML, Markdown, or even paper, design a browsing interface that allows a visitor to:

  1. See all ten fruits at a glance (thumbnail grid)
  2. Filter by one category (e.g., "show only citrus fruits" or "show only red fruits")
  3. Navigate between entries in sequence
  4. Jump to a specific entry from the tag index

Test your prototype with a friend or family member. Can they find what they are looking for? Do they discover unexpected connections? Use their feedback to refine your design before building the final exhibition.
