Submission Instructions

Upload the ZIP to the LMS submission portal, and place a short README inside the archive that lists the files included, the sources of the images (if not original), and one sentence about what you learned from the exercise.

AI supports your learning, it does not replace your intellectual work. You are accountable for understanding and explaining all submitted material. Implicitly claiming AI-generated content as your own work constitutes academic misconduct and insufficient authorship.

  • Permitted: AI for brainstorming, drafting ideas, refining phrasing, and checking grammar or clarity. Crucial: Critically evaluate, modify, and fully understand any AI output you integrate into your work.

  • Prohibited: Submitting AI-generated text without thorough mental parsing and comprehension. Ask yourself: If interviewed, can you explain every choice and observation in your own words?

  • Permitted: AI for inspiration, mood boards, layout ideas, colour suggestions, and rough visual concepts. Crucial: Adapt, refine, justify, and understand any AI-assisted visual choices you include.

  • Prohibited: Submitting AI-generated images or designs as your own without meaningful revision and understanding. Ask yourself: If interviewed, can you explain your visual decisions and how each element supports your work?

Academic Integrity Declaration

I declare that, except where I have referenced, the work I am submitting for this assessment task is my own work. I have read and am aware of my institution's Academic Integrity Policy and Procedure.
