# Module 8.3: Activities

## Resources

<section>

### Photographing Food and Natural Objects (Chen & Ibrahim, 2022)

This is the same reading introduced in Module 1, but now you can read it with the experience of having photographed fruits across multiple modules. Pay particular attention to the sections on lighting consistency and creating visual narratives through image sequences.

Access [Photographing Food and Natural Objects](https://orangeuni.edu/library)

</section>

<section>

### Colour Management for Food Photography (Larson, 2021)

This practical guide covers colour calibration, white balance, and post-processing techniques for food photography. It includes step-by-step instructions for setting up a consistent colour workflow using free or low-cost software.

Access [Colour Management for Food Photography](https://orangeuni.edu/library)

</section>

<section>

### Exhibition Photography: From Documentation to Art (Kowalski, 2023)

Also mentioned in Module 6, this guide's second half addresses exhibition photography specifically. It covers curation through images, sequencing photographs to create narrative, and balancing consistency with variety across a collection.

Access [Documenting Change](https://orangeuni.edu/library)

</section>

## Practice Activity

Plan and execute a photography session for your Assessment 3 exhibition:

1. Decide on a consistent visual approach (background, lighting, angles, colour treatment).
2. Photograph two of your ten exhibition fruits using this approach.
3. Review the results with your exhibition partner. Do the photographs communicate what you want them to? Are they consistent with each other?
4. Refine your approach based on feedback and photograph the remaining fruits.

Keep notes on your setup so you can replicate it if you need to reshoot any fruit later.
