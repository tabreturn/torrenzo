`=[[outline]].assessment.a3`

## Task Summary

Working in pairs, curate a digital fruit exhibition featuring ten tropical fruits. Each entry must include a photograph, sensory description, cultural or botanical notes, and consistent metadata. Submit the exhibition as a styled HTML page or Markdown document with a 500-word curator's reflection.

---

## Context

Digital exhibitions allow curators to share collections with a global audience. This assessment brings together everything you have learned about observation, documentation, tagging, and visual presentation. Your exhibition should tell a story -- not just a list of fruits, but a curated journey that reveals connections between sensory experience, cultural significance, and botanical diversity.

---

## Task Instructions

1. **Form a pair** with a classmate. Divide responsibilities for research, photography, writing, and design.
2. **Select ten tropical fruits** that represent diversity in colour, texture, origin, and cultural significance. At least three fruits should be from outside the citrus and stone fruit families covered in early modules.
3. **Create an entry for each fruit** containing:
   - A high-quality photograph (original or properly attributed)
   - A 100--150 word sensory description
   - Cultural or botanical notes (origin, traditional uses, interesting facts)
   - Consistent metadata tags (family, origin region, season, flavour profile)
4. **Design the exhibition** as a single HTML page or styled Markdown document with:
   - A title and introductory curator's statement (150 words)
   - A consistent visual layout with clear navigation between entries
   - A searchable tag index or filter system
   - Proper image attribution throughout
5. **Write a 500-word curator's reflection** explaining your selection rationale, curatorial decisions, and what you learned about digital collection presentation.
6. **Submit** the exhibition file, all assets, and reflection as a ZIP archive.

---

## Submission Instructions [[cc-section]]

Submit a ZIP archive named `ass_03_digital_exhibition.zip` containing:
- `exhibition.html` or `exhibition.md` (your main exhibition file)
- `assets/` folder with all images
- `reflection.md` (your curator's reflection)
- `README.md` listing all files and attributions

Upload to the LMS submission portal. Only one submission per pair is required; include both student names and IDs in the README.

---

## Referencing [[cc-section]]

Image sources must be attributed in the exhibition itself (captions or footer). Written sources should be cited in APA 7th edition within the reflection and exhibition text where appropriate. A minimum of six sources is expected across the pair's research.

---

## Academic Integrity Declaration [[cc-section]]

We declare that, except where we have referenced, the work we are submitting is our own. Both group members have contributed substantially to the research, writing, and design of this exhibition. We have kept records of our collaborative process and can share them if required.
