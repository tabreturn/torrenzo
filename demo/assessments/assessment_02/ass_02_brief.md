## Task Summary

Write a 1200-word comparative report analysing two tropical fruits using structured observation frameworks. Your report should include a comparison table, sensory descriptions, and a reflection on how preservation methods affect each fruit's characteristics.

---

## Context

Comparing fruits side by side reveals patterns that single observations might miss. This assessment builds on the catalogue skills you developed in Assessment 1 by asking you to go deeper: how do two fruits differ in structure, flavour development, and response to preservation? Structured comparison is a fundamental skill in food science, botany, and product development, and it sharpens your ability to notice and articulate subtle differences.

---

## Task Instructions

1. **Select two tropical fruits** that share some characteristics but differ in others (e.g., mango vs papaya, lychee vs rambutan, jackfruit vs durian).
2. **Observe and document** each fruit using the sensory framework introduced in Modules 4-6. Record appearance, aroma, texture, flavour profile, and structural features.
3. **Create a comparison table** with at least six dimensions (e.g., skin texture, flesh colour, sweetness level, acidity, seed structure, seasonal availability).
4. **Apply one preservation method** to a sample of each fruit (drying, freezing, or sugar preservation) and document the before-and-after characteristics with photographs and notes.
5. **Write a 1200-word report** structured as follows:
   - Introduction (150 words): rationale for your fruit selection
   - Individual profiles (300 words each): detailed sensory descriptions
   - Comparison section (300 words): analysis using your comparison table
   - Preservation reflection (150 words): how each fruit responded to preservation

---

Submit a single PDF document containing your report, comparison table, and before-and-after photographs. Name your file `ass_02_comparative_analysis.pdf` and upload to the LMS submission portal.
